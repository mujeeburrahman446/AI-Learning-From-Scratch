## 8. Practical skill to develop
Learn to connect advanced theory with real engineering decisions:
- what model size makes sense?
- what training strategy is efficient?
- what inference setup will meet latency and cost goals?
- how do we deploy safely?

## Summary
Advanced theory and practice turn AI learning into engineering judgment. That is the transition from learning about AI to building competitive AI systems.
